# M2.1 shared harmonic tension

The pad and arpeggiators use the same chord tones under the existing
root progression. The sustained drone remains a pedal tone. Set
`composition.tension` in DINAD's panel or set `tension` under
`[composition]` in project TOML. The three levels are:

- **0** uses the original major triad.
- **1** adds a minor seventh.
- **2** adds a minor seventh and lowers the fifth by a semitone.

DINAD applies changes to newly scheduled notes. A pad already sounding
continues until its scheduled release. The seeded arpeggio and resolved
take replay remain deterministic. The level is a voicing choice. It
does not measure dissonance against active voices or effect tails.

For the Fedora check, use the M1.6 layered panel and compare levels 0,
1, and 2 through at least one chord change each. Save and replay a take
containing the changes. This increment leaves voice and tail analysis,
and Lua controller automation, for later M2 work.
